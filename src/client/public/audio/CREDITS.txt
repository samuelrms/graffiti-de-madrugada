Foley samples in this folder (footsteps, landing, jump, climb rustle, explosion crunch,
punch) come from Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero
(CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Packs used: Impact Sounds, Sci-Fi Sounds, RPG Audio. Files renamed; no other changes.

Everything else is synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API:
paint weapons and splats, spray hiss, UI, stingers, the lo-fi music loop and the
night-city ambience (src/client/audio/).
